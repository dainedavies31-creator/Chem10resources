Lesson 1

Nomenclature of organic compounds and Isomerism

Steps to find the IUPAC name of a compound

  • Step 1: Find the longest carbon chain.
  • Step 2: Find the position number of alkyl branches, double or triple bonds (if any), and functional groups.
  • Step 3: Write the position number and name of branch. (Skip if there are no branches.)
  • Step 4: Write the position number and name of halogens or ether groups.
  • Step 5: Write 'cyclo' if the compound is cyclic.
  • Step 6: Write the word root.
  • Step 7: Write the position number and name of the bonds (position number not required for single bonds).
  • Step 8: Write the position number and name of functional groups.

Step 1: Finding the longest carbon chain

Finding the longest carbon chain is the most important step in naming a carbon compound. Everything else is related to the main chain.
